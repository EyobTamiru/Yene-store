## The approved description

Yene Business Group can develop customized ERP-style business systems. When
describing this work, always use exactly this wording, on a single line:

> **"An ERP-style system customized by Yene Business Group for the customer's business."**

## What not to do

**Do not describe it as a copy, clone or version of another ERP product.**
Each system is built for the specific customer and their own requirements, so
comparing it to a named third-party product is inaccurate as well as
disrespectful to the customer who paid for it built.

Do not name or imply a competing ERP system as the basis for the work. If a
customer asks whether it is based on a particular product, explain that it is
customized for their business by Yene Business Group.

## Which packages can include it

- **Platinum** can include a custom inventory management system developed by
  Yene Business Group.
- **Super** can include more extensive custom development, depending on the
  customer's requirements.
Note the wording: these packages **can include** custom work. Whether a
particular business actually has it depends on what was agreed and configured
for that business. Do not tell a customer they have a custom ERP unless it is
confirmed on their account.

For a custom system, connect the customer with Yene Business Group to discuss
requirements and scope.
