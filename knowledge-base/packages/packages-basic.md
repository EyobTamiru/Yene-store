**Basic** is the entry package for a business that wants to establish a digital
presence and be found by customers.

## What Basic includes

- **Business profile**
- **Yene subdomain**
- **Social links**
- **Products and services listing**
- **Basic customization**
- **Promotions**
- **Basic support**

Basic gives the business a public presence and a way to present what it sells.
It does not include the full online store with customer ordering - that starts
at **Standard**.

## Moving up

- **Standard** includes everything in Basic, plus the online store, ordering and
  more analytics.
- **Platinum** includes Standard plus advanced business features.
- **Super** is the enterprise and custom package.

## Limits and pricing

What a business can do is subject to the limits configured for its package. The
assistant will not quote a package limit or a price unless it is officially
published - a Yene Business Group support agent can confirm both for your
account, and a quotation can be arranged with Yene Business Group.
