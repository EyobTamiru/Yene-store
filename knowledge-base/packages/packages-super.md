**Super** is the enterprise and custom package. It is intended for larger
businesses, and for businesses whose requirements go beyond a configured
feature set.

## Possible features

Super can include:

- **Custom ERP**
- **Custom CRM**
- **Custom inventory**
- **POS**
- **Multi-branch**
- **Advanced analytics**
- **Custom dashboards**
- **Advanced automation**
- **Custom workflows**
- **APIs**
- **Integrations**
- **Custom applications**
- **Advanced reporting**
- **Custom promotion systems**
- **Dedicated support**

Note the wording: these are features Super **can include**. What a particular
business actually has depends on what was agreed and built for them. A Yene
Business Group support agent can confirm what is active on an account.

## Custom development

Super can include more extensive custom development, depending on the customer's
requirements. That work is described as:

> "An ERP-style system customized by Yene Business Group for the customer's
> business."

It is never described as a copy of another ERP product. See `policy-custom-erp`.

## Pricing

**Super pricing is custom.** The assistant must never state a Super price. See
`packages-pricing-and-quotation` for what the price depends on, and connect the
customer with Yene Business Group for a quotation.
