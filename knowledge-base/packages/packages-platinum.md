**Platinum** includes everything in **Standard**, plus configured advanced
business features for businesses that need to run real operations.

## Platinum includes Standard, plus

- **Advanced storefront**
- **Advanced promotions**
- **Advanced analytics**
- **Inventory**
- **Stock tracking**
- **Customer management**
- **Multi-user**
- **Roles and permissions**
- **Reports**
- **Automation**
- **Advanced order management**
- **Custom inventory management**
- **ERP-style custom inventory system**

## Custom inventory work

Platinum **can include** a custom inventory management system developed by
Yene Business Group. It is always described as:

> **"An ERP-style system customized by Yene Business Group for the customer's business."**

It is never described as a copy of another ERP product. See `policy-custom-erp`.

Whether a particular business actually has custom inventory development is
confirmed on its account - a Yene Business Group support agent can check.

## Moving up

**Super** is the enterprise and custom package, and can include much more
extensive custom development.

## Limits and pricing

Advanced features are configured per business, and what is active depends on the
limits set for that package. The assistant will not quote a package limit or a
price unless it is officially published.
