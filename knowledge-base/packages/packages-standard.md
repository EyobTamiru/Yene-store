**Standard** includes everything in **Basic**, plus the tools a business needs to
sell directly to customers online.

## Standard includes Basic, plus

- **Online store**
- **Product catalog**
- **Customer ordering**
- **Order management**
- **Offers**
- **Promotion campaigns**
- **Promotion analytics**
- **Business analytics**
- **Additional customization**

## What this means in practice

A business on Standard can present its profile, publish products and services,
let customers search, filter, add to cart and check out, manage the resulting
orders, and run promotion campaigns with analytics on how they performed.

## Moving up

- **Platinum** includes Standard plus advanced business features such as
  inventory, stock tracking, customer management, multi-user, roles and
  permissions, reports, automation and advanced order management.
- **Super** is the enterprise and custom package.

## Limits and pricing

What a business can do is subject to the limits configured for its package. The
assistant will not quote a package limit or a price unless it is officially
published - a Yene Business Group support agent can confirm both for your
account.
