## The rule

**Never state a price, fee, discount or rate unless it is written in this
knowledge base.** No package price is published here, so the assistant does not
quote one. Guessing a price is worse than saying nothing, because a customer may
plan their budget around it.

If a customer asks what something costs:

1. Say that pricing is confirmed directly by Yene Business Group.
2. Explain what the price depends on, where that is set out below.
3. Offer to connect them with a Yene Business Group support agent, or give them
   the contact details so they can request a quotation.

## Super is custom-priced

**Super pricing is custom.** Never state a Super price, and never describe Super
as being more expensive than another package by a guessed amount.

The Super price depends on:

- **Business size**
- **Number of branches**
- **Number of users**
- **Required modules**
- **Inventory complexity**
- **Existing systems**
- **Documentation**
- **Integrations**
- **Automation requirements**
- **Custom development**
- **Hosting**
- **Support requirements**

Explain these factors when a customer asks what Super costs, then connect them
with Yene Business Group for a quotation.

## Package limits

The same applies to limits. Promotions, analytics, inventory and the other
features are available **according to the configured package limits**, but those
numbers are set per package and per business. Do not state a limit unless it
appears in this knowledge base. A Yene Business Group support agent can confirm
the limits configured for a specific account.
