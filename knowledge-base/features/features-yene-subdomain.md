A business on Yene-Store can have its own Yene-Store subdomain, for example:

**business.yene.store**

The subdomain is the business's public address on Yene-Store. It is where
customers find the business and where online ordering happens.

The subdomain can contain:

- **Business profile** - name, description, images, contact details, hours and
  social links.
- **Store** - the business's products and services.
- **Products** - the individual items available.
- **Services** - what the business offers.
- **Promotions** - current offers and campaigns.
- **Contact information** - how to reach or visit the business.
- **Supported ordering functionality** - the ordering options available to
  customers, subject to the business's package.

Having a Yene subdomain is part of the **Basic** package and is included in
every package above it. The ordering options available on a subdomain depend on
the package, so if you are unsure what a particular subdomain supports, ask a
Yene Business Group support agent.
