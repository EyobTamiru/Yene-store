Inventory is how a business knows what it holds, where it is, and what needs
restocking. Inventory and stock tracking are part of the **Platinum** package.

## What inventory covers

- **Products** and the quantities held for them.
- **Stock** levels and **stock tracking**.
- **Stock movements** - everything going in and out.
- **Adjustments** - correcting the recorded quantity.
- **Low-stock alerts** - being told when an item needs reordering.
- **Warehouses** - storing stock in more than one place.
- **Branches** - running the business from several locations.
- **Suppliers** and **purchasing**, including procurement.
- **Inventory reports** for reviewing what is held and how it is moving.

## Custom inventory systems

Where a package includes custom development, the inventory system can be built
as **an ERP-style system customized by Yene Business Group for the customer's
business**. See `policy-custom-erp` for exactly what that means and what the
assistant may and may not say about it.

## Live figures

Current quantities, low-stock thresholds and alerts are specific to your
account and change continuously, so the assistant will not quote them from
memory. A Yene Business Group support agent can confirm them for you.
