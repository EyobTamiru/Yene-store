Customer management gives a business a single view of the people who buy from
it, so the business can serve them better.

Yene-Store covers:

- **Customer profiles** - the details held about each customer.
- **Customer history** - what the customer has done before.
- **Orders** - the orders a customer has placed.
- **Communication** - staying in touch with customers.
- **Customer activity** - what customers are doing now.

Customer management is part of the **Platinum** package, and customer data also
feeds the business analytics available from **Standard** upwards.

What the assistant can tell you is limited to your own account, and to
information you are authorised to see. For a specific customer's record, a
Yene Business Group support agent can help.
