The business profile is the public face of a business on Yene-Store. It is the
first thing a customer sees and the foundation everything else is presented
against.

A business profile can include:

- **Business name** - the name customers know the business by.
- **Logo** - the business's own mark.
- **Cover image** - a banner image representing the business.
- **Description** - a summary of what the business does.
- **Category** - the type of business, so customers can find it.
- **Contact information** - how customers reach the business.
- **Location** - where the business is based.
- **Business hours** - when the business is open.
- **Products** - what the business sells.
- **Services** - what the business offers.
- **Social links** - the business's social media pages.

A complete profile with products, services, contact details and social links is
included from the **Basic** package, and is carried through the higher packages.
See `packages-basic`.

If you want help completing or improving a profile, a Yene Business Group
support agent can advise.
