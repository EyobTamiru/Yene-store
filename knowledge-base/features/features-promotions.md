Promotions let a business offer products and services at special terms and
promote them to customers.

Promotions are available across Yene-Store packages **according to the
configured package limits**. What a particular business can create depends on
the package it is on, so if you need to know your exact promotion limits, ask a
Yene Business Group support agent.

## What a business can create promotions for

- **Products**
- **Services**
- **Discounts**
- **New products**
- **Events**
- **Seasonal campaigns**
- **Special offers**

## Promotions and packages

- **Basic** includes promotions.
- **Standard** adds offers, promotion campaigns and promotion analytics.
- **Platinum** adds advanced promotions.
- **Super** can include custom promotion systems.

Because the limits are set per package, the assistant will not guess what a
given business is allowed to run. A support agent can confirm the configured
limits for your account.
