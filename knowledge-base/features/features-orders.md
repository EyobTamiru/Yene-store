Orders cover the whole path from a customer deciding to buy, through to the
business dealing with the resulting order.

## Creating an order

A customer creates an order from the business's store by adding items to the
cart and checking out. A business user can also create an order on behalf of a
customer.

## Order status

Every order has a status that reflects where it is in the process. Order
management lets a business review its orders and work through them.

## Order management

- Review incoming and past orders.
- See the **customer information** attached to each order.
- See **delivery and payment information where supported**, depending on the
  package and what the business has configured.

## Checking the status of your own order

The live status of a particular order is specific to your account and changes
over time, so the assistant will not state it from memory. Sign in and a support
agent can confirm the current status, or a Yene Business Group support agent can
check it for you.
