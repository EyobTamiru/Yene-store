Business management is the operational side of Yene-Store: the people, the
permissions, the systems and the money.

## People and access

- **Users** and **employees** who work in the business.
- **Roles** and **permissions** that decide what each person can see and do.

Multi-user access with roles and permissions is part of the **Platinum**
package.

## Operations

- **CRM** - managing customers and relationships.
- **Inventory** - stock, warehouses, branches and suppliers.
- **POS** - point of sale.
- **Procurement** and **suppliers** - buying stock and supplies.
- **Expenses** - tracking what the business spends.
- **Reports** - reviewing performance.
- **Multi-branch management** - running several branches as one business.

## Packages

- **Platinum** includes multi-user, roles and permissions, reports and
  automation.
- **Super** can include custom ERP, custom CRM, POS, multi-branch, custom
  dashboards, custom workflows, APIs, integrations, custom applications and
  advanced reporting.

Which of these apply to a business depends on its package and, for Super, on
what has been agreed for that business. A Yene Business Group support agent can
confirm what is active on your account.
