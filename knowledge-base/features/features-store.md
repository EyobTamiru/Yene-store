The store is where a business lists what it sells and customers browse and
order. The full online store is part of the **Standard** package and above.

## What a store contains

- **Products** with images, prices and descriptions.
- **Categories** to organise the products.
- **Product images** so customers can see what they are buying.
- **Prices** and **descriptions** for each product.
- **Stock** information, and **variants** such as size or colour where a product
  comes in different options.

## What customers can do

- **Search** for products.
- **Filter** the results to narrow down what they want.
- **Add items to the cart**.
- **Check out** and place an order.

## Managing your store

A business adds and edits products, sets prices and descriptions, organises
categories and manages stock from its Yene-Store account.

Live details such as the current price of a product or how much stock is left
are specific to your business and change over time. A support agent can confirm
the current values for your account.
