A mall is a real shopping centre that sellers can register their stores under.
If your business trades from a mall, your YENE-STORE store is filed under that
mall, and customers can browse the mall and see every store registered inside
it.

## How registration works

At sign-up you choose **Own store** or **Mall store**. Choose **Mall store** and
you pick:

- the **mall** you trade from,
- the **floor** you are on,
- your **room or shop number**.

You can only register under a mall that is already on YENE-STORE. If your mall
is not in the list, contact a Yene Business Group support agent and ask for it
to be added.

## Trading from more than one room

If you occupy more than one counter, add every room number - each one is listed
on your storefront and in the mall directory, so customers can find you at any of
them. One store holding two rooms is still one store.

Each room belongs to one seller. If a room is already registered to another
store in that mall, YENE-STORE will ask you to choose a different one.

## Browsing by mall

The marketplace has a **Shop by mall** section listing each mall, how many
stores are registered in it, and every store with its floor and rooms. Opening a
mall shows all of its stores, and also the rooms that are still unclaimed if you
are looking to register.

## Your YENE subdomain

A mall covered by a YENE-STORE mall agreement grants its registered stores a
free YENE subdomain. Stores in a mall without an agreement follow the normal
package rules for a subdomain. See `features-yene-subdomain`.

## What a shopper sees

On your storefront, the mall name, your floor and all of your room numbers are
shown on every product page and store card, so a customer always knows which
counter to come to.
