Analytics shows a business how it is performing, so decisions are made on facts
rather than impressions.

Business analytics are part of the **Standard** package.

## What analytics cover

- **Sales**
- **Orders**
- **Revenue**
- **Products** - which ones perform
- **Customers**
- **Inventory**
- **Promotions**, including **promotion analytics** to see how a campaign did
- **Business performance** overall

## Packages

- **Standard** includes business analytics and promotion analytics.
- **Platinum** includes advanced analytics.
- **Super** can include advanced analytics and custom dashboards.

Live figures - revenue, sales totals, rankings - are specific to your account
and change over time, so the assistant will not state them from memory. Open the
analytics in your account, or ask a Yene Business Group support agent.
