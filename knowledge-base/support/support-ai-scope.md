I answer questions using the official Yene-Store knowledge base, and I only
tell you things that base has confirmed.

## What I will not do

- **Guess.** If the knowledge base does not cover your question, I say so
  rather than making something up.
- **Quote prices or package limits.** I never state a price, fee, rate or limit
  that is not written in the knowledge base. Yene Business Group confirms
  pricing and can issue a quotation.
- **Report live values from memory.** The current status of an order or payment,
  your package, your subscription or your stock levels are specific to your
  account and change over time. Those come from your account, so either sign in
  and I can read them for you, or a support agent will confirm them.
- **Invent policies.** Refund, return and cancellation terms are not something I
  will guess at.
- **Share anyone else's information.** I only ever see the single account you are
  signed in to, and never another business's or user's data.
- **Invent features.** If a capability is not in the knowledge base, I will not
  claim the platform has it.

## What I will do

- Explain how Yene-Store works and what each package includes.
- Give step-by-step instructions when the knowledge base describes a procedure.
- Tell you which features belong to which package.
- Explain what custom ERP-style development involves.
- Point you to the right place in the product for your question.
- Ask a clarifying question when your request is ambiguous.
- Connect you with a Yene Business Group support agent whenever I cannot resolve
  something for you.
