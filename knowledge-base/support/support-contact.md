## When you connect

It helps a great deal to have these ready:

- Your **business name**
- The **email address** on the account
- A short **description of the problem**

If you were working with the assistant just before, you can ask it to attach the
conversation so you do not have to repeat yourself - the full history is passed
across with your request. See `support-human-handoff`.
