Yene-Store is a digital business platform developed by Yene Business Group.

It helps a business establish a digital presence, sell its products and
services, promote itself, manage day-to-day operations, automate workflows and
grow.

## The core idea

Yene-Store follows a simple progression:

**BUILD → DIGITIZE → SELL → AUTOMATE → MANAGE → GROW**

1. **Build** - create the business's digital presence.
2. **Digitize** - move the business's information and catalogue online.
3. **Sell** - take orders online through the Yene-Store subdomain.
4. **Automate** - reduce manual work by automating workflows.
5. **Manage** - run operations, stock, staff and customers in one place.
6. **Grow** - use analytics to see what is working and act on it.

## Who Yene-Store is for

- **Businesses** that want to sell products or services online and manage their
  operations.
- **Business owners** who need control over their profile, store, staff and
  reporting.
- **Business employees** who work with orders, stock and customers day to day.
- **Customers** of those businesses, who order through the Yene subdomain.

Yene Business Group also develops custom ERP-style business systems. See
`policy-custom-erp` for what that means and which packages it applies to.
